01 / About

KUFU HIVE

A workshop for ideas worth exploring.

Kufu means creative ingenuity — the thoughtful little improvements that turn a question into something useful, surprising, or fun.

Kufu Hive is an independent lab. Projects begin as small web experiments and puzzles; the ones that resonate can grow into full applications. Different worlds, connected by a common curiosity.
