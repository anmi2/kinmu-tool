Request account deletion

To request deletion of your NextGuess account and its associated personal information, email app.guess.number13@gmail.com from the email registered to your account. Include your user ID if available. Do not send your password.

Important

This page provides a contact channel; it does not itself perform deletion. Where required, an in-app account-deletion option must also be provided.
